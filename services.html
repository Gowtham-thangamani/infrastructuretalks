Infrastructure Engineering Services

End-to-end infrastructure engineering across Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah — delivered by DAS & Partners, the UAE's #1-ranked infrastructure consultancy.

What We Deliver

Full-scope infrastructure design and authority approvals, taken from concept to construction supervision under one accountable team.

Roads & Highways

Highway geometry, interchange and junction design, pavement engineering and signalised junctions, with full RTA and municipality approvals for arterial and community road networks.

Storm Water & Drainage

Hydraulic modelling, water-flow analysis and optimal drainage routing engineered to municipality and RTA standards — protecting roads and communities from flooding.

Pipeline Design

Potable water, sewerage, irrigation and district-cooling distribution pipelines, coordinated with roadworks and site development into a single infrastructure package.

Utilities Networks

Full wet and dry utilities — water, sewerage, drainage, electrical distribution and telecom ducting — coordinated with DEWA, ADDC, SEWA, Etisalat and du.

Traffic Diversions

Temporary and permanent traffic-diversion planning, mobility studies, approved drawings and on-site construction supervision that keep works moving without disrupting public mobility.

Marine & Coastal Works

Engineering for sensitive coastal environments — corrosion-resistant design, waterfront utilities and complex utility crossings for the region's marine developments.

Bridges & Structures

Structural design for bridges, culverts, underpasses and retaining structures, integrated with the road and drainage packages for seamless delivery.

Authority Approvals & NOC

End-to-end authority approvals and NOC permitting across RTA, DEWA, ADDC, SEWA, Dubai Municipality, DDA, Trakhees and Abu Dhabi's DMT — delivering first-time approvals.
