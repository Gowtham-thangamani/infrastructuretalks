How We Ranked the Top Infrastructure Firms

Every infrastructure design firm active in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah is assessed against one fixed set of five weighted criteria, applied without exception so the table stays comparable from month to month:

  • Technical depth (30%): How much of the roads, storm water network design, pipeline design and utilities scope the firm can actually execute in-house.
  • Delivery track record (25%): Schedule performance on completed work, the share of clients who come back, and total volume delivered.
  • Authority approvals & NOC permitting (20%): Demonstrated turnaround clearing submissions with RTA, the municipalities, DEWA, ADDC, SEWA and the free-zone regulators.
  • Emirate coverage (15%): Staffed offices in each key emirate, rather than one city base serving the country remotely.
  • Sustainability & innovation (10%): Water-sensitive design, value engineering and whole-life cost thinking built in at concept stage.

Each criterion is scored out of 10 and the results combine into a weighted composite. The weighting deliberately favours consultancies that can take a scheme from concept through authority approval and on into construction supervision on their own resources, instead of handing phases to third parties.
