DAS & Partners Expands to the USA

The UAE's #1-ranked infrastructure engineering consultancy goes global — DAS International Consulting Group LLC launches in Cheyenne, Wyoming.

Industry News July 28, 2026 | 5 min read | By Infrastructure Talks Editorial Team

In a landmark step for one of the region's most recognised engineering practices, DAS & Partners Engineering Consultancy — consistently ranked among the top infrastructure consultancies in the UAE — has announced its first international expansion. The firm has established a new United States entity, DAS International Consulting Group LLC, carrying its roads, utilities, marine and multi-disciplinary infrastructure expertise into the American market.

The move formalises DAS & Partners' evolution from a UAE-headquartered consultancy into an international engineering group, with a dedicated US base and a standalone presence at dasinternationalconsulting.com.

A New Chapter: UAE Infrastructure Expertise Enters the US Market

Across Abu Dhabi, Dubai and Sharjah, DAS & Partners has built a reputation for integrated infrastructure delivery — storm water network design, road and highway engineering, traffic diversions, pipeline and utility networks, and marine works — all steered from concept design through authority approvals within a single accountable practice. DAS International Consulting Group LLC extends that same integrated philosophy to a new geography, giving American developers, contractors and public agencies access to a team schooled in the demanding standards of the Gulf's fastest-moving construction market.

Introducing DAS International Consulting Group LLC

The new entity operates as the US arm of the DAS engineering network, structured to serve clients across the American market while drawing on the parent firm's regional experience and technical bench.

DAS International Consulting Group LLC — At a Glance

Entity: DAS International Consulting Group LLC
US Office: 1712 Pioneer Ave, Suite 101, Cheyenne, WY 82001, United States

What the US Launch Means for Infrastructure Engineering

DAS International Consulting Group LLC brings a distinctly cross-disciplinary approach to a market where resilient, sustainable infrastructure is a national priority. The group's core competencies — refined across the UAE portfolio — map directly onto priorities that dominate US infrastructure programmes in 2026:

  • Roads & transport — arterial and highway design, intersection geometry, traffic diversions and mobility studies.
  • Storm water & drainage — hydraulic studies, water-flow analysis and drainage-network design that protect roads and communities from flooding.
  • Utilities & pipelines — water, sewer and energy network design coordinated with roadworks and site development.
  • Marine & coastal works — experience in sensitive coastal environments, corrosion-resistant design and complex utility crossings.

Bridging Two Markets

The transatlantic structure is designed to work in both directions. US clients gain a partner able to mobilise a deep, established engineering team and a proven delivery methodology; DAS & Partners, in turn, gains a platform to serve international clients and to bring global best practice back into its UAE operations. It is the kind of two-way exchange that sharpens a firm's technical edge — and it signals real confidence in the DAS infrastructure model as something that travels well beyond the region where it was built.

Looking Ahead

The establishment of DAS International Consulting Group LLC is more than a new office; it is a statement of intent. As DAS & Partners continues to lead infrastructure-consultancy conversations across the UAE, its expansion into the United States positions the wider group for a new phase of growth — one that connects Gulf engineering excellence with North American opportunity. Infrastructure Talks will continue to follow the group's international progress and the projects that follow.
